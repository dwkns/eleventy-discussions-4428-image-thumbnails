Web accessibility ensures that websites and applications are usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. Creating accessible digital experiences is both an ethical responsibility and often a legal requirement.

## Why Accessibility Matters

Accessible design benefits everyone. Clear navigation, legible text, and keyboard support improve the experience for all users, not just those with disabilities. It's about inclusivity and reaching the widest possible audience.

## WCAG Guidelines

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) provide a set of recommendations for making web content accessible. These guidelines are organized around four principles: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, and Robust.

## Practical Steps

Start by using semantic HTML, implementing proper heading hierarchies, and ensuring sufficient color contrast. Add ARIA labels where needed, and always test your applications with keyboard navigation and screen readers.

## Testing for Accessibility

Regular accessibility testing throughout the development process catches issues early. Use automated tools, manual testing, and real user feedback from people with disabilities to ensure your applications are truly accessible.

Building accessible applications demonstrates professionalism and respect for all users.
